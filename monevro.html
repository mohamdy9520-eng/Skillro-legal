Monevro

An AI expense tracker and budget planner that helps you understand your spending and reach your savings goals.

Download for Android iOS – coming soon

What you can do with Monevro

Scan receipts with AI
Take a photo of a receipt and Monevro extracts the amount, date, and merchant for you.
Track your spending
Record your expenses and income and see where your money goes.
Plan budgets
Set budgets and get alerts before you go over them.
Reach your savings goals
Create financial goals and follow your progress over time.
Get AI-powered advice
Receive personalized financial tips and plans based on your own numbers.
Keep your account secure
Sign in with email or Google, and optionally use biometric login on your device.
